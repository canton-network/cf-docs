## Path parameters








<ParamField path="domain_id" type="string" required>


The synchronizer ID to look up traffic for.






</ParamField>



<ParamField path="member_id" type="string" required>


The participant or mediator whose traffic to look up, in the format `code::id::fingerprint` where `code` is `PAR` or `MED`.






</ParamField>







## Responses


### 200

ok

<ResponseField name="traffic_status" type="MemberTrafficStatus" required>

The current traffic state for the member on the synchronizer under `actual`, and the total purchased traffic under `target`. The purchased traffic may exceed the `actual` limit as purchases take time to be incorporated into the limit.





<Expandable title="child attributes">




<ResponseField name="actual" type="ActualMemberTrafficState" required>

The current traffic state for the member on the synchronizer





<Expandable title="child attributes">




<ResponseField name="total_consumed" type="integer (int64)" required>

Total extra traffic consumed by the member on the given synchronizer






</ResponseField>



<ResponseField name="total_limit" type="integer (int64)" required>

Current extra traffic limit set for the member on the given synchronizer. An extra traffic top-up is complete once total_limit matches total_purchased.






</ResponseField>




</Expandable>


</ResponseField>



<ResponseField name="target" type="TargetMemberTrafficState" required>

Total purchased traffic; may exceed limit in `actual`





<Expandable title="child attributes">




<ResponseField name="total_purchased" type="integer (int64)" required>

Total extra traffic purchased for the member on the given synchronizer in bytes.






</ResponseField>




</Expandable>


</ResponseField>
